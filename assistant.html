Recycling Assistant

Take or upload a photo of an item and the assistant will tell you if it can be recycled, and where.

This uses AI to identify items from a photo — it can get things wrong, especially on unusual, damaged, or unclear items. Treat its answer as a helpful guess, not a guarantee, and use the Recycling Guide if you want the definitive rules.
