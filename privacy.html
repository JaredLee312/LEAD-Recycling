Privacy Policy

What BinFinderSG collects, why, and how to access, update, or delete it.

BinFinderSG is a student-built project (not a government service — see the footer disclaimer on every page), but it still handles real data from real people, so we hold ourselves to the same standard a production site would. This page explains what we collect, why, and your rights under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Both are handled entirely by Supabase Auth, a managed identity provider — we never see, store, or touch your password ourselves. Creating an account also generates a two-factor authentication (TOTP) secret, which Supabase Auth stores, not us.
  • Bin reports (only if you submit one): which bin, the type of issue, an optional written description, a required photo, when you submitted it, and a link to your account so you can find, edit, or delete it later.
  • Recycling log entries (only if you log one): a material type, a quantity, and a timestamp. These are not linked to your account — they feed an anonymous, aggregated community total, and we have no way to trace an entry back to who submitted it.
  • Recycling Assistant photos: not collected at all. The photo you show it is analyzed entirely on your own device using a general-purpose image-recognition model — it's never uploaded anywhere, including to us, and no account is needed to use it.
  • We never ask for your name, phone number, home address, or any other profile information — browsing, searching, and reading the recycling guide never requires an account at all.

How "find a bin near you" actually works

BinFinderSG does not use your device's GPS location or your IP address to figure out where you are, and your browser is never asked to share your location. Instead, the town name you type is matched against a small, fixed list of Singapore town coordinates built into the site, and the on-page bin list is sorted by distance from that town's coordinates — not from you. We don't know, request, or store your physical location at any point.

Why we collect what we do

An account exists for one reason: to prevent anonymous spam on the two actions that write shared, public data — reporting a bin issue and logging a recycling entry. That's it. When you create an account, the sign-up form requires you to actively check a box agreeing to this policy — we don't assume consent just because you typed an email in.

How long we keep it

  • Bin reports and their photos are kept until you remove them yourself via My Reports, or until we delete them after a verified account-deletion request (see below).
  • Reports also stop appearing on the public bin-list pages after 24 hours — that only hides old reports from other visitors, it does not delete them, and they stay visible to you on My Reports indefinitely.
  • Recycling log entries can't be traced to an account, so they're kept indefinitely as part of the community's running totals.
  • We don't currently auto-delete accounts that go inactive. If you want your account and its data gone, contact us and we'll do it manually — see below.

How we protect it

  • Passwords are never handled, stored, or hashed by our own code — Supabase Auth owns that entirely, end to end.
  • Every account requires two-factor authentication (a 6-digit code from an authenticator app) in addition to a password.
  • Access is enforced by Row Level Security rules at the database level — even someone bypassing this website entirely and talking to the database directly still could not read, edit, or delete another user's report. This is a database guarantee, not just a website-level check.
  • All traffic to and from this site is encrypted (HTTPS) — both the website itself and the database it talks to.
  • The API key visible in this site's public source code is a restricted "publishable" key by design; it cannot bypass the database rules above, unlike a private key, and is safe to be public.

Your rights, and how to use them

  • Access, update, or delete a bin report: sign in and go to My Reports at any time — no need to contact anyone.
  • Update your password, change your email, or delete your account entirely: we don't yet have a self-service page for this, so email bensimjy@gmail.com and we'll action verified requests directly, within 30 days.
  • Stop using the site: you can do this at any time — no account is required to browse bins, the recycling guide, or public analytics.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect or why ever changes, this page will be updated to match. Last updated: 5 August 2026.
